2003 - The Beginning

Born in the Philippines to parents who were OFW's. Before I turned 5, we embarked on a journey across continents.

2003-2008 - Africa Calling

At age 3, we immigrated to Africa, beginning in Eswatini. By age 5, we moved to South Africa, discovering new cultures and technology.

2008-2016 - Discovery

South Africa became where I discovered my love for computer science and coding. For 8 years, I learned how computers work.

2016-2020 - Namibia Years

We moved to Namibia where I completed most of my high school career and solidified my technical skills.

2020-Present - Canada

In 2020, we moved to Canada. I'm now pursuing Computer Science at Okanagan College, fascinated by data structures and algorithms.
