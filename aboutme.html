My Story

I was born on the year 2003 in the Phillipines. My parents were OFW's (Overseas Filipino Workers), which meant that we moved to another continent before I was 5 years old. At the age of 3 we immgrated to Africa where I spent majority of my formative years as well as my childhood and teen years. We moved to Eswathini (then called Swaziland) and when I was 5 years old, we moved to South Africa, its next door neighbor. It was there where I discovered my love for computer science and coding. We stayed there for 8 or so years and then moved to Namibia where I finished most of my highschool career. In 2020, we moved to Canada and it was here where my love for computer science fully bloomed. I wasnt so interested in making new programs, more so in the manner which data is stored and how such large quantities of data can be stored in the smallest of devices with efficient ways to retrieve data using code. This led me to pursue a Computer Science course at Okanagan College where I am currently a student.
